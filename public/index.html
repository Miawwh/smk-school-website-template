VISION

To become a leading vocational high school that produces competent, well-charactered graduates ready to compete in the global era through mastery of technology and entrepreneurship

MISSION

We are committed to implementing an industry-based curriculum, building partnerships with the world of work, and developing an entrepreneurial spirit and student character through technology learning and holistic values

GOALS

To produce certified graduates with 85% absorption into the workforce or higher education, encourage 20% of alumni to become entrepreneurs, and become a pioneer of technology-based vocational schools with character education
