about the dkv program

The Visual Communication Design (DKV) program prepares students to become creative professionals who are skilled at conveying messages through visual elements. Here, students will learn fundamental design principles such as typography, color theory, layout, and illustration, as well as handle industry-standard software—from Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, to Figma and Sketch for interface design. The project-based curriculum covers branding, editorial design, advertising, packaging design, and UI/UX, so graduates are able to design aesthetic and effective visual communication solutions. Through studio practice, real client case studies, and cross-disciplinary collaboration, the DKV program hones creativity, conceptual skills, and presentation skills, preparing graduates to work as graphic designers, art directors, illustrators, or UI/UX designers across various creative industries

What Do You Learn in DKV?

Typography & Color Theory

Understand typography principles, visual hierarchy, and color theory to create aesthetic and readable compositions across various print and digital media

Illustration & Vector

Master drawing techniques and create vector illustrations using industry-standard software (Adobe Illustrator, CorelDRAW) for branding, editorial, and packaging needs

UI/UX & Interactive Design

Design user interfaces (UI) and user experiences (UX) with wireframing, prototyping (Figma, Sketch), and usability principles for web and mobile applications

Facilities in the DKV Program

  • Complete Design Studio

    Workstations with high-performance iMac / graphics PCs, Wacom graphics tablets, and ergonomic chairs for comfortable creative work

  • Licensed Software

    Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), CorelDRAW, Figma, and Sketch for 2D–3D design and prototyping

  • Print & Mockup Lab

    Large format printers, cutting plotters, and screen printing machines to produce posters, packaging, and physical portfolios

  • Design Library

    A collection of books, magazines, and online access to design journals/articles as references on typography theory, color, and creative trends

Career Opportunities for DKV Graduates

Graphic Designer

Create visual designs for print/digital media such as posters, brochures, and branding

Salary: IDR 4-12 Million
*Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator & Photoshop
Illustrator

Create digital illustrations for books, comics, merchandise, and social media content

Salary: IDR 5-15 Million
*A strong portfolio is required
UI/UX Designer

Design user interfaces and digital experiences for apps & websites

Salary: IDR 6-20 Million
*Proficiency in Figma/Adobe XD
Motion Designer

Create 2D/3D animations for ads, video content, and visual presentations

Salary: IDR 5-18 Million
*Proficiency in After Effects & Blender
Product Designer

Design physical/packaging products considering aesthetics & functionality

Salary: IDR 5-15 Million
*Proficiency in 3D modeling (optional)
