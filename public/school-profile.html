Open Full Profile

School History

SMK Inovasi Digital Manado Vocational High School was founded in 2018 with the aim of providing quality vocational education that is relevant to the development of information and communication technology. The school is committed to producing graduates ready to face the challenges of the working world in the digital era

meaning of the school logo

The Tut Wuri Handayani logo, with its burning torch symbol and blue circle, represents the spirit of education that enlightens. Tut Wuri Handayani (from behind giving encouragement) is the philosophy of Ki Hajar Dewantara which emphasizes the role of educators in motivating students to think independently. The torch symbolizes knowledge, its flame as the light of truth, while the blue circle depicts the world of education that is whole and limitless.

VISION

To become a leading vocational high school that produces competent, well-charactered graduates ready to compete in the global era through mastery of technology and entrepreneurship

MISSION

We are committed to implementing an industry-based curriculum, building partnerships with the world of work, and developing an entrepreneurial spirit and student character through technology learning and holistic values

GOALS

To produce certified graduates with 85% absorption into the workforce or higher education, encourage 20% of alumni to become entrepreneurs, and become a pioneer of technology-based vocational schools with character education

School Facilities

Computer Lab

An air-conditioned room with 150 latest-generation i7 PC units, supporting computer network, programming, and graphic design practice

Fiber Optic Lab

Hands-on fiber optic network installation practice with professional tools: fusion splicer, OTDR and fiber tester

Digital Library

Access to 10,000+ e-books and online journals through 20 dedicated computers with a discussion room that seats 250 students

Basketball Court

A FIBA-standard court with special flooring and LED lighting for both training and official matches

Auditorium

A multipurpose hall with a capacity of 756 people, professional acoustic system, and complete multimedia equipment

Healthy Canteen

Serving nutritionally balanced meals with a digital payment system and a dining area with a capacity of 500 people
