TKJ Students of SMK Yadika Manado Successfully Complete the 2025 UKK

Tuesday, 22 April 2024
By Admin
News / Activities

(Manado, 22 April 2024) – The networking team of SMK Inovasi Digital Manado won 1st Place in the Student Skills Competition (LKS) in the field of IT Network Systems Administration at the North Sulawesi Province level. This achievement was earned after fierce competition against 25 teams from vocational high schools across the province in server troubleshooting trials, firewall security configuration, and multi-site network deployment simulations.

The team, consisting of Kevin Susanto (XII TKJ) and Putri Rahayu (XII TKJ), outperformed the other participants with a perfect score in the critical network recovery session. Under the guidance of coach Mr. Rudi Hermawan, S.Kom., they solved data breach and DDoS attack scenarios through a layered defense strategy based on MikroTik and Cisco IOS. "This victory proves our students' readiness to face real digital infrastructure challenges," said Principal Diana Wulandari, S.Pd., M.Kom., in a virtual press conference.
