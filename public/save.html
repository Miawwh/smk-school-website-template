TKJ Students of SMK Yadika Manado Successfully Take the 2025 UKK

Tuesday, March 12, 2024
By Admin Editorial Team
News / Activities

Students of the Computer and Network Engineering (TKJ) department at SMK Yadika Manado achieved an outstanding accomplishment in the 2025 Competency Exam (UKK). The two-day activity was attended by all grade XII students of the TKJ department, with full support from supervising teachers and the school.

On the first day, students took the hands-on practical exam at the school's computer laboratory. Each participant was tested on network installation, server configuration, and hardware troubleshooting. The Head of the TKJ Department, Mr. Andi Wijaya, explained that preparation had been carried out since the beginning of the semester with exam simulations and in-depth practical material.

"We provide intensive training, from simple network installation to Linux-based server management," said Mr. Andi. "It's great to see the students' enthusiasm. Some of them have already completed the skill modules faster than expected."

"Our target is not just to pass, but also to master ready-to-use industry skills. Because in the working world, practical experience is very important."

Andi Wijaya, Head of TKJ Department

On the second day, it was time for the theory exam, which lasted three hours in the classroom. The questions covered computer networks, basic programming, and cybersecurity. The majority of students were able to complete the questions with satisfactory scores. The final results showed that the average score of TKJ students at SMK Yadika Manado increased by 10% compared to the previous year's UKK.

Meanwhile, the Chairperson of the 2025 UKK Committee, Mrs. Ratna Dewi, added that the school also invited several IT practitioners from the local industry to provide additional material. This was done so that students would be better prepared to face the challenges of the working world and understand the latest technology trends.

With these satisfying results, it is hoped that TKJ graduates of SMK Yadika Manado can be accepted directly for internships or jobs at partner companies, as well as having the opportunity to continue their education to a higher level in the IT field.
