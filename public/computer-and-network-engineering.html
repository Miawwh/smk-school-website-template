about the tkj program

The Computer and Network Engineering (TKJ) program prepares students to become experts in designing, building, and managing reliable network infrastructure and computer systems. Here, students will learn the full-stack management of networks — from system requirements analysis, network device installation, server configuration, cybersecurity, to infrastructure maintenance — through hands-on practice with Cisco and MikroTik devices and realistic network simulations. With an industry-based curriculum and collaborative projects, the TKJ program hones technical skills, troubleshooting logic, and digital security awareness, so graduates are ready to become competent network technicians, system administrators, or cybersecurity experts in the era of digital transformation.

What Do You Learn in TKJ?

Computer Networks

Designing & configuring LAN/WAN networks, routing, switching, and troubleshooting with Cisco & MikroTik devices

Cybersecurity

Securing networks from cyber attacks, malware analysis, and firewall implementation (pfSense, Cisco ASA)

Server Administration

Installation & configuration of Linux/Windows servers (DNS, DHCP, Web Server) and virtualization with VMware

Facilities in the TKJ Program

  • Enterprise Network Lab

    Cisco 2900 Series Routers, MikroTik CRS354 Layer 3 Switches, and GNS3 simulator for realistic network practice

  • Professional Simulators

    Cisco Packet Tracer, EVE-NG, and Wireshark for network traffic analysis

  • Cybersecurity Lab

    Dedicated infrastructure for ethical hacking & firewall practice (pfSense, Cisco ASA)

  • Industry Certifications

    Certified training for Cisco CCNA, MikroTik MTCNA, and CompTIA Security+

Network Infrastructure Projects by TKJ Students
